3D printing

FeatureRule of thumbDetails
Overhangs45° from horizontal self-supports in most processes; flatter needs support (not SLS / MJF)Overhangs
Bridges (FDM)5-10 mm spans are reliable; longer spans sagOverhangs
Horizontal holesTeardrop or diamond top so the hole prints without supportOverhangs
Down-facing edges45° chamfer prints clean; a fillet on the underside sagsOverhangs
Escape holes (powder)At least two holes, about 3.5-5 mm, at opposite ends of a hollowPassages
SLA hollow partsDrain hole plus vent hole to stop suction and trapped resinPassages
DMLS channelsTeardrop or diamond section above about 8 mm (0.30 in) diameterPassages
Fits / clearanceFDM 0.2-0.5 mm, SLA 0.1-0.2 mm, SLS / MJF 0.3-0.5 mm per sideTips
HolesPrint undersize; drill or ream critical holes afterwardTips
ThreadsHeat-set inserts or tapped holes; model threads only when coarse and largeTips
Cost driversZ height (SLA, DMLS), part volume and nesting (SLS, MJF), support volume (FDM, SLA, DMLS)Tips
