Corners & pockets

New designs often call out a corner radius that exactly matches a cutter, such as R0.125 for a 1/4 in end mill. The tool then has to stop and pivot in the corner, so the shop finishes it with a smaller, slower cutter. Draw the corner a little larger than the cutter radius (about 1.3 x) and give the floor a standard bullnose fillet smaller than the wall radius (the picker suggests one; sharp floors are not accepted in hard metals). See Inside Corners & Pockets for the reasoning and diagrams.

Part mass & drill finder

Tip

Drill sizes: fractional, number and letter drills, plus metric drills in 0.1 mm steps to 10 mm and 0.5 mm steps above. Designing hole diameters to those increments lets the shop use a stock drill instead of interpolating with an end mill. For threaded holes, use the thread pilot hole tables or tap drill charts instead.

Threads & holes
