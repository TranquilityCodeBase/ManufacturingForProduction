Resin parts age

Most SLA resins keep curing in sunlight, get more brittle and slowly creep under load. Use them for prototypes and test parts, not long-life loaded parts, unless the resin is rated for it.

Plan for post-processing

DMLS parts are stress relieved, cut from the plate, and support-stripped. Leave machining stock (about 0.25-0.5 mm / 0.010-0.020 in) on sealing faces, bores and threads, and call those faces out on the drawing.

Temperature, UV and chemical notes

  • Heat: compare the material's heat deflection temperature (HDT) at your load, not its melting point. A part under load softens well below its melt.
  • UV: resins and PLA yellow and embrittle in sunlight. Paint, dye or choose ASA / nylon for outdoor use.
  • Moisture: nylons absorb water and change size and stiffness slightly. PA12 absorbs less than PA6 or FDM nylon.
  • Chemicals: nylons handle oils, fuels and most solvents well. ABS and many resins are attacked by solvents.
  • Food / medical: needs a certified material, process and post-processing chain. Printed porosity can trap contamination.
