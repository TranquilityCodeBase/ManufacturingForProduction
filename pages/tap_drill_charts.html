Standard shop tap drills and clearance holes for Unified inch and ISO metric screws. Use these when holes are tapped conventionally or for clearance holes in mating parts.

At a glance
  • 75% threadAluminum, brass, plastics
  • 50-60% threadSteel, stainless, iron (easier tapping, little strength loss)
  • Close fitAccurate location, dowel-like screw fit
  • Free fitGeneral assembly, allows positional tolerance

Which column do I use?

Tap drill vs clearance drill

Tap drill = the diameter you model for a threaded hole (with a cosmetic thread). 75% thread is the common default; tougher materials tap more reliably near 50-60% with only a small loss of strip strength. Clearance drill = the hole in the part the screw passes through. Free fit is the safe default; close fit only when the screw also locates the parts. Automated CNC services may want a slightly different pilot: check the thread pilot hole tables.

Unified inch threads (UNC / UNF / UNEF)

Dimensions in inches. Clearance drills depend on screw size only, so they repeat for each pitch.

ISO metric threads

Dimensions in millimetres with the closest inch drill. Coarse pitch is the default; fine pitches are for thin walls, adjustment screws and vibration resistance.
