Holes & Threads

Thread Pilot Hole Tables

The hole diameter to model for each thread when the part is quoted by an automated CNC service such as ProtoLabs. The software reads the plain hole diameter and offers the matching threads; it never needs modeled thread geometry.

How to use these tables

1. Model a plain hole at the Modeled pilot dia (any value between Min and Max works). 2. Keep the thread length at or below Max thread depth, measured from the top of the surrounding features. 3. Assign the thread in the quote (or call it out on the drawing). Obstructions, lead-in chamfers or fixturing can reduce the depth actually available.

Vendor specific

These ranges are tuned to one vendor's thread mills. If your shop taps holes with standard taps, use the tap drill charts. Vendor tables change, so re-check the linked source page before release.

Standard threaded holes (milled)

Right-hand internal threads cut with a thread mill. UNC and UNF from #2 to 1/2 in, metric from M2 to M12 (M1.6 in soft metals only). Hole axes must line up with one of the part's cardinal machining directions.

Coil insert threaded holes

Holes cut with the oversize STI thread for HeliCoil-style coil inserts. The thread listed is the screw size the installed insert accepts. The vendor cuts the thread; you supply and install the insert.

Key insert threaded holes

Holes for key-locking inserts. The thread listed is the fitting thread cut in the part for the insert's outside thread; match it to the insert maker's data sheet. Key-locking inserts also need a counterbore for the keys.

Special threads: NPT pipe threads

Tapered pipe threads for fluid and air fittings. Model a straight pilot hole at the listed diameter with at least the listed pilot depth.

External (OD) threads on turned parts

External threads are cut on the lathe. Model the plain shaft at the listed diameter, with no thread geometry, and choose the thread in the quote. Maximum threaded length is about twice the nominal diameter.
