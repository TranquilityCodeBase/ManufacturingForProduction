# Tap & Clearance Drill Chart - Metric (ISO)

Shop tap drills and clearance drills for ISO metric screws, with the closest inch drill. For automated CNC quoting use [[Threads_ProtoLabs_Metric|ProtoLabs Metric]] pilot ranges instead.

## Quick Rules
- Coarse-pitch 75% tap drill is about **nominal - pitch** (M6 x 1.0 -> 5.0 mm).
- 75% for aluminum, brass, plastics; 50% for steel, stainless, iron.
- Close fit for location, normal fit for general assembly.
- Prefer coarse pitch unless vibration, thin walls or fine adjustment need fine pitch.
