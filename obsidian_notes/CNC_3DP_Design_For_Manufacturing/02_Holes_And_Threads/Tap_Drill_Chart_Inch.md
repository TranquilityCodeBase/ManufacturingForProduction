# Tap & Clearance Drill Chart - Inch (UNC / UNF / UNEF)

Shop tap drills and clearance drills for Unified inch screws. Use when you or your shop will drill and tap. For automated CNC quoting use [[Threads_ProtoLabs_UNC|ProtoLabs UNC]] / [[Threads_ProtoLabs_UNF|UNF]] pilot ranges instead.

## Quick Rules
- **75% thread** tap drill: aluminum, brass, plastics (more engagement, easy to tap).
- **50% thread** tap drill: steel, stainless, iron (less tap torque, fewer broken taps; strength loss is small).
- **Close fit** clearance: accurate location, little float. **Free fit**: easier assembly, more float.
- In CAD, model the tap drill diameter and add a cosmetic thread. See [[Threads_Design_Rules]].
